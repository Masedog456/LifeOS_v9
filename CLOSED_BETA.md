# LifeOS — Closed Beta

Welcome, and thank you for trying LifeOS early. This page is for **you, the beta
user**. It's written in plain language — you don't need to know anything about how
LifeOS is built.

## What this beta is

LifeOS is a calm place to **capture what you're thinking and reading, and turn it
into knowledge you can trust and return to.** You can jot a thought, add something
you're reading, highlight and annotate it, ask questions grounded in the source,
and save what's useful — on your own device and (when you sign in) synced securely
to your private account.

This is an **early closed beta**. It is stable enough for real use, but it is not
finished. You are helping us find the rough edges before a wider release.

## Who it's for

A small, invited group of people who:

- like to think in writing,
- read things they want to remember and act on, and
- are comfortable using an early product and telling us what's confusing or broken.

## What to try (a good first session)

1. Open the app. The first screen asks **"What's on your mind?"** — write a thought
   and press **Capture**.
2. Reload the page. Your capture is still there.
3. Go to **Learn → Reading** and **＋ Add reading**. Paste an article, or upload a
   PDF / text file.
4. Open it and read. Try **highlighting** a sentence and adding a **note**.
5. Open **✦ Ask & study** and ask a question about the document. Notice that answers
   cite the passage they came from — and say honestly when the document doesn't
   cover something.
6. **Save** something useful back into LifeOS.
7. Use **Search** (the search box, or ⌘K / Ctrl-K) to find what you captured
   earlier.
8. If you sign in, try opening LifeOS on a second device and confirm your work is
   there.

## Signing in

Signing in is by **email link** (a one-time magic link — no password). Sign-in
enables **secure sync** across your devices. Until you sign in, LifeOS works fully
on the device you're using and shows **"Saved locally."**

## Known limitations (on purpose, for now)

- **Word (.docx)** files aren't read inside LifeOS yet — paste the text instead.
- **Scanned PDFs** (images of pages) can't have their text extracted yet; LifeOS
  tells you honestly instead of guessing.
- The AI **Ask / Summarize** features only use the passages of the document you're
  reading — never your whole library, and never outside knowledge dressed up as if
  it came from the document. If no AI provider is configured, you'll get an honest
  on-device draft answer instead.
- LifeOS does **not** use end-to-end encryption and never claims to.

## Supported browsers

Best experience on the **current version** of:

- **Desktop:** Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari.
- **Mobile:** Safari (iPhone), Chrome (Android).

Older browsers may work but aren't a focus during the beta.

## Your privacy

- Your captures, readings, notes, and files are **yours**. When you're signed in,
  they live in **your own private account** and are protected so that **no other
  user can see them**.
- Uploading a file and **reading it** happen on your device. Only **Ask** and
  **Summarize** send the *relevant passages of the one open document* to the AI
  provider — never your whole library.
- We do **not** track your activity for advertising, and we don't want your
  sensitive content in bug reports. When you report something, please **don't paste
  private content** unless it's necessary to explain the problem — and only if
  you're comfortable sharing it.

## How to report a problem or an idea

Use the **Send feedback** link in the app (under **More / Help**) — or reply to
your beta invitation. When you write, it helps to include:

- what you were trying to do,
- what you expected,
- what happened instead,
- your browser + device (e.g. "iPhone Safari", "Chrome on Windows").

## ⚠️ If you think you lost data — tell us immediately

LifeOS is designed so a capture is **saved on your device the moment you press
Capture**, even if syncing is unavailable, and a sync problem should **never delete
your local work.** If you ever believe something you created **disappeared**:

1. **Don't panic and don't sign out** — signing out/in is fine, but leave the app
   as it is if you can.
2. **Report it right away** via Send feedback (or your invite thread), with the
   word **"DATA LOSS"** and roughly when it happened and on which device/browser.

We treat any suspected data loss as the **most serious** kind of beta report and
will look at it first.
