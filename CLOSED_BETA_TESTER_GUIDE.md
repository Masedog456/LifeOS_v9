# Conqify — closed beta

Thanks for trying this. You are one of a small number of people using it.

**Please do not read a manual first.** We want to know what Conqify is like when
nobody explains it to you. If something is confusing, that is the finding — not
a mistake you made.

---

## Use it for a few days

Not a checklist. Just things worth trying at some point.

- Capture something messy — a half-thought, a worry, something you don't want to lose.
- Figure out what actually matters today.
- Add something you want to remember later.
- Try the **Constitution Builder**.
- Attach a source — a book or a note — to something you are working out.
- Hide something from AI.
- Come back tomorrow and see whether it still makes sense.

Use real things if you are comfortable doing so, and don't if you aren't.
Nothing you write is sent anywhere unless you deliberately send it.

---

## What Conqify records during the beta

Plainly, because you should be able to check it:

- **It records counts.** How many questions you answered, whether you adopted or
  dismissed a suggestion, whether you hid something from AI.
- **It never records what you wrote.** Not your answers, not your Constitution,
  not your notes, not your sources. The record has no field those could go in.
- **It never records your name or email.**
- **All of it stays in your browser.** Nothing is uploaded, synced, or included
  in your export.
- **If you send beta feedback**, only what you type in that box is shared, and
  only when you send it.
- **Resetting your local data or signing out deletes all of it.**

You can read the whole record yourself at `/dev/beta-evidence`.

## Afterwards, tell us

Six questions. Short answers are fine — a sentence each is plenty.

1. What confused you?
2. What felt unexpectedly useful?
3. What did you expect Conqify to do that it did not?
4. Was anything shown or changed that surprised you?
5. Did anything feel invasive?
6. What would make you stop using it?

Use **Send beta feedback** on the Help page, or reply however you normally reach us.

---

## One thing to tell us immediately

If anything ever appears in your Constitution that **you did not explicitly add**,
stop and tell us straight away. That should be impossible, and if it happens we
want to know before you do anything else.
